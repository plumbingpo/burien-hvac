Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Burien

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Burien HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Burien and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Burien HVAC program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation before peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
